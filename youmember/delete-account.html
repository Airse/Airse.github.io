Request account deletion

You can ask us to permanently delete your you Member? account and the personal data associated with it.

How to send your request

  1. Select the button below to open an email with a request template.
  2. Send it from the same email address you used to register for you Member? with Google.
  3. We’ll use that address to verify the request and contact you if we need anything else.
Email a deletion request
Before you send: The request must come from the email address registered to your account. Please don’t include your password, card numbers, barcodes, or attachments in the email.

What happens next

We’ll verify that the request comes from the account holder, then delete the account and associated data in accordance with our privacy policy. Data that must be retained for a legitimate legal or security reason will be limited and handled as described in that policy.
